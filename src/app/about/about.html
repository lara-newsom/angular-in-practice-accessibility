At The Every Hotel, we believe in providing a seamless and accessible experience for all our guests. Located in the heart of the city, our hotel combines modern amenities with timeless hospitality to ensure your stay is as comfortable as it is memorable.

What are the check-in and check-out times? +

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out options are available upon request and subject to availability.

Is parking available at the hotel? +

Yes, we offer secure on-site valet parking for $35 per night with unlimited in and out privileges. Self-parking is also available in the adjacent garage.

Do you offer accessible rooms? +

Absolutely. We have a variety of ADA-compliant rooms featuring roll-in showers, grab bars, and visual alarm notifications. Please mention your specific needs when booking.

Is the hotel pet-friendly? +

Yes, we love pets! We welcome up to two pets per room with a non-refundable cleaning fee of $50 per stay. Service animals are exempt from fees
